# Kilo's sounds: where they came from

Every file here is mono, 16-bit, 22050 Hz. Each was cut from a recording on
Wikimedia Commons that its author released into the public domain or under
CC0 (no conditions), so nothing here needs attribution or carries a
share-alike condition. Fetched 2026-11-19. Each cut was high-pass filtered
(120 Hz; 60 Hz for the purr), faded in and out, and levelled.

The cuts were chosen from each file's description and its loudness over
time, not by ear. To replace one, put a WAV of the same name and format
here and rebuild the kernel.
